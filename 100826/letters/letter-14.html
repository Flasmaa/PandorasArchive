To: My Dearest Daughter


Today marks a special day not only for you but also for me. I gave birth to you and you made me a mother. Since then, your heartbeat has changed my life.

You are my first baby, the one who made me a mom. No matter how many birthdays come and go, a part of me will always remember the little girl who first called me “mama,” and how that little girl changed my whole world. Being your mom has been one of the greatest gifts of my life. You were the beginning of so many things for me, the beginning of my worries, my sleepless nights, my prayers, my sacrifices, and most especially, a kind of love I never knew could exist.

I know I may not always be the perfect mom. There are days when I worry too much, remind you too much, or become a little too protective. But behind every reminder, every sacrifice, every little thing I do for you is a mother who simply wants to see her daughter happy, safe, loved, and fulfilled.

I hope you always remember that you don't have to be perfect to make me proud. You don't have to achieve everything in life for me to love you. I am already proud of the person you are and the person you are becoming.

As you grow older, I hope you continue to be kind, brave, humble, and true to yourself. Chase your dreams, make mistakes, learn from them, laugh loudly, love deeply, and never be afraid to become the woman you are meant to be. And whenever life feels heavy, I hope you remember that you will always have a home in me. No matter how old you get, no matter where life takes you, you will always be my first baby.

Thank you for making me a mother, Xy. You were my first experience of a love that is unconditional, fierce, and forever.

Happy birthday, anak!
Mama loves you more than words can ever say. I am, and will always be, so proud to be your mom.


From: Mama
